About Me

I am a senior in Computer Science (Minor in Mathematics) at the University of Michigan (GPA: 3.94/4.0), applying to PhD programs in Quantum Computing for Fall 2027.

My research interests lie at the intersection of quantum compilation, fault-tolerant surface-code quantum computing, and efficient classical simulation of quantum systems. I am currently an undergraduate researcher in the CAFQA Lab under Prof. Gokul Ravi, focusing on dynamic lattice-surgery compilers for fault-tolerant architectures.
