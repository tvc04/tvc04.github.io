Quantum Computing · Classical Simulation

Efficient Classical Simulation of Large-Scale Unitary Cluster Jastrow Circuits

Hrishikesh Belagali, Thomas Van Camp, R. Pradeep, Sourin Das, Namit Anand, Ryan LaRose

This project investigated efficient classical simulation techniques for large-scale Unitary Cluster Jastrow (LUCJ) circuits. I conducted this research through the NSF-sponsored Advanced Computational Research Experience (ACRES) REU under Dr. Ryan LaRose. (Award #2349002)

I developed a pipeline for converting target molecular systems into quantum circuits and investigated their simulation using matrix product state methods. I also optimized GPU-based simulation workflows on ICER's high-performance computing cluster using Python's Qiskit, Quimb, PySCF, and ffsim libraries.

The work was presented as a poster and oral presentation at the 2026 Mid-SURE conference.
