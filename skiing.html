My Skiing Background

Beyond quantum computing, skiing is a defining passion of mine. I have had the privilege of skiing at world-class resorts across North America and Europe. Below are some of the places I've explored.

Lake Louise (Alberta, Canada)

Located in Banff National Park, Lake Louise offers stunning scenery and diverse terrain across four mountain faces.

Chamonix-Mont-Blanc (France)

A legendary destination for extreme skiing at the foot of Mont Blanc. The Vallée Blanche descent is a highlight.

Kitzbühel (Austria)

Home of the infamous Streif downhill race, Kitzbühel offers classic Austrian Alps charm and vast intermediates terrain.

Beaver Creek (Colorado, U.S.A.)

Known for its upscale amenities and impeccably groomed runs, Beaver Creek is a fantastic Rocky Mountain experience.

Vail (Colorado, U.S.A.)

One of the largest ski resorts in the world, famous for its expansive Front Side and legendary Back Bowls.

Mammoth Mountain (California, U.S.A.)

Boasting high altitude and a long season, Mammoth offers excellent terrain variance in the Eastern Sierra.

Blue Mountain (Ontario, Canada)

A popular destination in Ontario, offering diverse runs and a vibrant village atmosphere.
